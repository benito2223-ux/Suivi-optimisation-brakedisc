**Visit report – Stellantis Sept-Fons – 9 and 10 November 2026**

**Contact:** Matis Hernu (process / optimisation), with the line team (operators, line supervisor)
**Topic:** Finishing trials on brake discs – SPK SL500 insert, EMAG 2 and HESSAPP lines

**Summary**
Two good days on site. We ran two trials, collected wear and roughness data, and agreed a clear action list with Matis. The first result on EMAG 2 is positive. The HESSAPP trial is not.

**What we did**
- EMAG 2, DV 356 disc, OP40 (finishing): trial with SNGN 090416 F in SL500, Vc 700, f 0.25, G96. Operator: Léandre. Raw discs 4 days old, shot-blasted 1 day before. 60 discs machined.
- HESSAPP, 302x26 RPI disc, OP30 (right-hand lathe): trial with SNGN 090408 F in SL500 using the competitor's cutting parameters (Vc 675, f 0.32), to compare on the same basis. 60 discs measured.
- Talked with Julien about tool T6401 and the burr created in the inner diameter.
- Talked with Aymeric (line supervisor) about how Ra is measured today.

**Results**
- EMAG 2: the target tool life (50 discs) was reached. Runout stayed well inside the 0.04 mm limit up to disc 50. Ra was just at the limit at the end of the tool life, with no runout problem and no chipping. Wear is a comb-type wear with high flank wear, so the insert has no margin left on this batch. Result: positive.
- Hot discs and recent shot-blasting seem to play a role. We want to repeat the trial with fresh and with mature raw discs and keep the used inserts for wear analysis.
- HESSAPP: not conclusive. Comb wear appears early and Ra rises clearly from disc 30. Runout stayed inside the limit. Trial closed as out of tolerance and the scenario is set to abandoned.
- Ra is measured on paper at the end of the tool life and written by hand. On PCI, about one disc in three is above the Ra tolerance at the end of the tool life. This is a good argument for a regular and recorded Ra check.

**Customer feedback**
- The team is open and motivated. Matis is now using the tool daily and filling in the data himself.
- Raw disc maturity matters for them. We now record the casting day and the tool calculates the age of the raw discs.
